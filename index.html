Privacy Policy

Ashdod Museum Kiosk App
Last updated: August 17, 2026

Summary

This app is an interactive kiosk experience that runs on iPads installed at a museum exhibition. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data collection

The app collects no data of any kind. It requires no account or login, has no analytics or tracking, and makes no network connections — all content (videos, images, and text) is bundled inside the app and displayed locally on the device.

Camera

The app uses the iPad's rear camera solely to display a live preview on screen as a visual background during video playback. The camera feed is shown in real time only; it is never recorded, stored, analyzed, or transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data, no personal information is collected from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated accordingly.
